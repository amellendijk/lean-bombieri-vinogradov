\chapter{The two external inputs}\label{ch:inputs}

Two analytic results are quoted from the literature and taken as axioms in Lean. Both are stated
with an unspecified constant, which is itself part of the axiom, and in accordance with
\Cref{not:asymptotic} every implied constant in this document is allowed to depend on these two
constants. A statement whose proof invokes one of the two inputs, directly or through an earlier
result, lists it among the results its proof uses; in this sense every estimate from
\Cref{cor:pnt} onwards should be read as beginning with ``Assume \Cref{thm:siegel-walfisz} and
\Cref{thm:large-sieve}''.
